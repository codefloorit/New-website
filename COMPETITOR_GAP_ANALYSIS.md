# Codefloor IT — Competitor and SERP Gap Analysis

Date: 6 October 2026.

## Method and limits

Four web searches were run for Ahmedabad queries and the result sets were reviewed: who ranks, what type of page ranks, and how titles are built. This is a qualitative read of the results.

Not available, and therefore not claimed here: search volumes, keyword difficulty, backlink counts, competitor traffic, or Codefloor IT's current rankings. Those need Google Search Console plus a tool such as Ahrefs or Semrush. No competitor content was copied.

## What the results look like

| Query | Who ranks | Page type that wins |
| --- | --- | --- |
| web development company in Ahmedabad | Directories and list articles (DesignRush, Feedbax, TechBehemoths, dev.to "top 10" posts), then long-established agencies (AddWeb Solution, PerfectionGeeks, Plethora IT, Expert Web Designing) | "Top N companies" lists; agency location landing pages |
| custom web application development company Ahmedabad | Agency profiles on Clutch, Craft, Elioplus; agencies such as WebPlanex, Kunsh Technologies, Sapphire Software Solutions, CodeCustoms | Third-party profile pages; agency service pages |
| hotel booking website development company Ahmedabad | Mostly DesignRush category pages and generic pages; one programmatic "hotel booking software in Vadodara / Rajkot" site | Almost nothing specific to Ahmedabad hotels |
| website development cost in Ahmedabad | Cost pages with explicit rupee ranges per project type; "how long does a website take" timeline articles | Pages that publish numbers |

Observed patterns:

- **Title structure**: "[Service] Company in Ahmedabad | Brand", often with "Top" or "Best" and a year.
- **Trust signals competitors lean on**: years in business (12 to 20+), ISO certification, review counts on Clutch and DesignRush, large portfolios. Codefloor IT, founded in 2025, cannot match these and should not imitate them.
- **Directories dominate head terms.** For the broad query, more than half the visible results are lists and directories rather than agency sites.
- **Cost pages publish numbers.** The ranking cost pages give price ranges by project type.
- **Niche plus location queries are thinly served.** The hotel booking query returned no strong Ahmedabad-specific page.

## Where Codefloor IT can realistically win first

1. **Niche plus location pages backed by real projects**: hotels and resorts now, clinics once the appointment system is live. Competition is thin and the proof is genuine.
2. **Honest, specific guides** that answer one question well. These are what AI assistants quote.
3. **Brand and local presence**: Google Business Profile and directory listings. Directories hold the head terms, so being on them matters more than trying to outrank them.

## What not to do

- Do not publish "top 10 web development companies in Ahmedabad" lists that rank Codefloor IT first.
- Do not generate one page per city or per suburb with swapped place names.
- Do not add "best" or "#1" to titles, or imply years of experience, client counts or certifications the company does not have.
